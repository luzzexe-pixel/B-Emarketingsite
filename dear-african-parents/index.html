The game

Questions that sound like home.

Dear African Parents is a deck of beautiful questions about who we are, where we come from and the childhood so many of us quietly shared, whichever country, language or kitchen it happened in.

Pass it round at the dinner table, on a long drive, at a family gathering or on a first date. There are no winners. There is only the moment someone says, “wait, yours too?”

Personality

Who you are when no one is performing.

Culture

Traditions, tastes, proverbs and pride.

Childhood

The rules, rituals and phrases we all knew.

Family

The ones who raised us, and the ones who still do.

Sound familiar?

One childhood, a thousand kitchens.

  • “Do you think money grows on trees?”
  • “Greet the elders first.”
  • “Who is this friend, and who are their parents?”
  • “Because I said so.”
  • “Is that how we do things at home?”
  • “Come and eat.”

How to play

Three steps. No rules to argue about.

  1. 1

    Draw

    Take the top card and read it aloud.

  2. 2

    Answer

    Everyone answers honestly, in whatever language feels right.

  3. 3

    Compare

    Find out what you share, and what makes you different.
