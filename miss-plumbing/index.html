Questions

Good to know.

How quickly can you get to me?

Many customers are seen the same day, some within the hour. Call or WhatsApp and you'll get a realistic arrival time straight away.

Will I know the cost before you start?

Always. Quotes are free and no-obligation, and you get a clear quote before any work begins. If we find something extra we talk it through with you first, so there are no surprises on the invoice.

Are you Gas Safe registered and insured?

Yes. Claire is Gas Safe registered for boiler and gas work and fully insured. Ask to see the ID card when she arrives.

Can I ask for a female plumber?

Miss Plumbing is run by Claire, so she is who you'll meet. Customers living alone, or booking help for an elderly relative, often tell us it makes all the difference.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

We're open 8am–8pm, seven days a week, for emergencies and planned work. Need help outside those hours? Call and ask.

Will you leave my home tidy?

That's the standard. Dust sheets down, the area cleared afterwards, and old parts taken away (recycled where possible).

Which areas do you cover?

Romford and nearby, including Gidea Park, Collier Row, Harold Wood, Hornchurch, Upminster, Rainham, Chadwell Heath, Ilford and Brentwood. Not sure? Ask and we'll tell you honestly.
