How to play

Easy rules. Zero chill.

Learn it in a minute. Argue about it for an hour. Perfect for game nights with family, friends and that one cousin who always wins.

  1. 01

    Draw a card

    Someone reads out a situation only an African child would survive. “Who told you to open the fridge?”

  2. 02

    Answer for the table

    Everyone secretly picks the answer they think fits you best. Honesty is encouraged. So is exaggeration.

  3. 03

    Reveal and roast

    Flip your answers together. Defend yourself. Call a relative for backup if needed.

  4. 04

    Score the wisdom

    Closest answers earn points. The player with the most “Because I said so” tokens wins the table.

    Who is Dear African Parents for?

    Anyone who grew up with African parents, or loves someone who did. It works for friends, families, couples and cousins.

    How many people can play?

    2 to 10 players. It is at its best around a table with 4 to 6 and snacks.

    Is it family friendly?

    Ages 16+ for the Core Deck because of the roasting. Mild language, maximum honesty.
