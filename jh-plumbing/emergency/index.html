Stop the leak while you wait

If anyone is in danger, call 999. If you smell gas, leave the property, do not use switches or flames and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

01

Turn off the water at the stopcock

Usually under the kitchen sink or near the front door. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Open the cold tap to drain what is left.

02

Switch off the boiler

If the leak is near the boiler or the heating system, turn the boiler off to avoid damage.

03

Catch the drips

Use bowls, buckets and towels. Move valuables and furniture out of the way.

04

Keep electrics away from water

If water is near sockets, lights or appliances, turn off the electricity at the consumer unit if you can do so safely, and do not touch anything wet. If there is danger, call 999.
