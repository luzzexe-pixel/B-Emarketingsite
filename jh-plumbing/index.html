Questions

Good questions. Straight answers.

Do you really come out the same day?

Often, yes. Customers describe Jim arriving within 15 to 30 minutes for leaks and the same day for boiler faults. It depends on the job and the day, so call and he will give you an honest time.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. You can check any gas engineer on the official Gas Safe Register, and you should. We link to it from this site.

Do I get a quote before work starts?

Always. Quotes are free and agreed before the job begins. If a bigger job needs a visit first, that is free too, and the quote comes in writing.

Which boilers do you work on?

Customers have had Vaillant and Baxi boilers repaired, serviced and replaced, among others. Tell us the make and age when you call.

Will you make a mess?

No. Customers mention cupboards put back, work areas cleaned and old parts taken away. That is how Jim works.

What areas do you cover?

Romford and the surrounding Havering and Essex area, including Harold Hill, Gidea Park, Collier Row, Hornchurch, Upminster, Rainham, Dagenham and Brentwood. Not sure? Ask.

Can I WhatsApp a photo for a quote?

Yes. WhatsApp us a photo of the problem and we will reply as soon as we can during opening hours.
