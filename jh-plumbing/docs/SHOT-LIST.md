# Photography & video shot list

Grade everything for the palette: deep blue shadows, warm brass highlights, true neutrals in the midtones, fine grain.
Bright phone photos are fine: they sit inside dark Harbour Blue frames with a brass hairline on the site.

## Must-have (replaces placeholders)

1. **Portrait of Jim.** Low-key light, dark navy or charcoal wall, a rim light catching the tools. Calm, confident. Also a circular crop for the hero ("Hi, I'm Jim").
2. **30-second intro video**, vertical or square, captions burned in or as a track. Jim says who he is, what he does, and that he answers the phone.
3. **Before/after pairs from real jobs**, same angle and height, 9:16 clips work best (boiler cupboard, radiator swap, tap fit, bathroom, leak repair). Ask the customer first.
4. **Van at blue hour** (the grey Vaillant-branded van from the Google photo, cleaner and lit) and an **organised tool roll from above on dark slate**.

## Nice-to-have

5. **Macro details lit like product photography against black:** water arcing from a brass spout, a soldered copper joint, beads on chrome, a pressure-gauge needle. These double as section dividers.
6. **"Spotless" shots:** a finished bathroom at dusk with lights on, dust sheets being lifted, shoe covers by the door, a wiped and hoovered work area.
7. **Boiler details:** a neat boiler cupboard, labelled pipework, a service sticker, the flue run. Real and tidy beats stock.
8. **Hero loop (10–12 seconds):** finished work in motion (water from a tap, shower with lit niche, chrome valves). Under 2 MB desktop, 1 MB mobile, with a portrait cut and a poster under 100 KB.
9. **"Watch us work" strip:** 6–10 short vertical clips (5–10 seconds, no talking needed), cross-posted to TikTok and Reels.

## Technical

- Shoot at least 1080p; export hero as MP4 (H.264) + WebM (VP9), no audio.
- Stills: send originals, we convert to AVIF/WebP. Name files by subject (`kitchen-leak-after.jpg`).
- Get written permission for any customer home, face or review shown.
